# “Connections” — music provenance and license

- **Title:** Connections
- **Author:** ULecture contributors, 2026
- **Source:** Original score and signal synthesis in `media/generate-music.py`.
- **Master:** `ULecture-music.wav`, 75 seconds, 48 kHz, stereo, 24-bit PCM.
- **Composition:** 96 BPM, synthesized sine pads, plucks, bass, kick and rim, with seeded noise hi-hats. Every note and percussion event is included in `music-score.json`.
- **Inputs:** No recording, sample library, downloaded music, singing, humming, spoken voice, voice model or external musical work is used. All sound is calculated from oscillators and a deterministic noise generator. No service was purchased.
