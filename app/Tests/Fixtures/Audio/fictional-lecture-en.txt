Welcome to our fictional lesson about learning. Working memory holds information while we reason. A clear example helps us connect new ideas with what we already know. Reading a short passage, writing a note, and explaining the idea in our own words can reveal what we understand. Today we compare two imaginary classrooms. In the first classroom, students copy every sentence. In the second classroom, students pause, ask questions, and summarize the main point. These examples are invented for testing this application. No real lecture or personal recording is used.
